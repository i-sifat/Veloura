### Editorial checks

- Scenarios stay in a playful community-theater/date-night voice.
- Twists escalate the premise, mystery, or emotional stakes rather than scripting graphic physical activity.
- No story uses coercive, degrading, or non-consensual framing.
- Free stories cover all three categories and multiple tiers; premium packs add intensity without changing the safety standard.
