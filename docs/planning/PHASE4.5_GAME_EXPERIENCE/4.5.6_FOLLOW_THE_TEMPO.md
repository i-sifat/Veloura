## 0. Rules

Same 11 rules as 4.5.1 §0. Additionally:

## 1. Why it exists

It is the only game with nothing to read during play. That makes it the strongest proof of the
cognitive-load contract, and it costs about a day because there is no content to write.

## 3. `PulseRing` motion

Beat period = `60 / bpm` seconds. Per beat the ring scales `1.00 -> 1.14 -> 1.00`, spending 35% of the
period expanding (easeOutQuad) and 65% relaxing (easeInQuad). The inner glow opacity tracks the same
curve between 0.18 and 0.42.

Drive it from **one** `AnimationController` whose duration is reset when the stage changes. Never
rebuild the widget per beat and never rebuild a subtree containing the app bar or CTA. Wrap the ring
in `RepaintBoundary` and animate with `AnimatedBuilder` over a cached child.

`lightImpact` on each beat, capped at 2 per second, routed through the shared haptics helper so the
Phase 7 setting mutes it.

## 4. Round design (90 seconds plus a hold)

```
stage 1   30s    60 bpm    label "SLOW"
stage 2   30s    92 bpm    label "BUILD"
stage 3   30s   128 bpm    label "FASTER"
finale     3s   frozen at max scale, label "HOLD", heavyImpact, ring glow rose @ 55%
```
Each stage draws one focus word shown in place of the stage label for its first 6 seconds, from a const
list: `HANDS · LIPS · NECK · SLOWER · CLOSER · EYES`. After that the stage label returns. That is the
entire content of this game and it lives in a Dart const, not a JSON pack.

On finale: `nextTurn()` and open a minimal result sheet — "Round complete" (`resultHero`) +
`PrimaryCta("Again")` + `SecondaryTextButton("Done")`.

## 6. Exit Gate

- [ ] The ring pulses at exactly the stage bpm (unit test: beat count over a simulated 30s stage).
- [ ] Stage transitions are seamless — no gap, no double beat, no jump in scale.
- [ ] Stopping mid-round cancels the ticker and haptics immediately with no lingering callbacks.
- [ ] Backgrounding pauses the round (`AppLifecycleState.paused`) and resumes cleanly.
- [ ] Reduced motion: the ring does not scale; it pulses colour between `rose @ 30%` and `rose @ 70%`.
- [ ] One hero, one CTA, at most one footnote, never more than two words of instruction.
- [ ] `Ticker` and `AnimationController` disposed — proven by a widget test that pumps and disposes.
- [ ] Worst frame time recorded on a low-end physical device (master plan §3 motion quality bar).
- [ ] analyze clean, build_runner clean, tests green.
- [ ] Commit + PR: `feat(tempo): follow the tempo pacing game`.
