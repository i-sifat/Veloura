# 4.5.7 — Passionate Roleplay: scenes, roles, beats

Blocked by: **4.5.1** and **Phase 4.1** (Roleplay engine + 40–50 story pack). Est: 1.5 days.
Read `4.5.0_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` first.

## 0. Rules

Same 11 rules as 4.5.1 §0. Additionally:
- **No new content.** `lib/features/roleplay/data/roleplay_seed.json` from Phase 4.1 is reused. Stories,
  roles, settings, goals, twist beats, categories, tiers, themed packs, premium gating and favourites
  are unchanged.
- If Phase 4.1 has not merged, **do not start** — there is no story pack to render.
- Twist beats stay **on demand**, never auto-timed (Phase 4.1 requirement).

## 1. Flow — three steps, one decision each

```
Step 1  Choose the scene      horizontal card carousel
Step 2  Assign roles          two role cards, one tap
Step 3  Play                  one beat + one "Next" CTA
```
No step shows more than one decision. Nothing scrolls except the carousel. All three steps live in one
route so Android back exits the scene once, not beat by beat (master plan Phase 9 navigation rule,
honoured early).

### Step 3 — play
```
App bar title = scene title, leading = close (X) 22
Beat dots under the app bar: one 6px dot per beat, active rose
Expanded > Center:
  beat line        resultHero, centred, padding H 28, maxLines 5
  12
  speaker chip     role name, cardLabel, textSecondary
PrimaryCta("Next")             -> next beat; on the last beat becomes "Finish"
SecondaryTextButton("Reveal a twist")   -> appends the next on-demand twist beat, max as authored
Transitions between beats: 220ms fade + 8px slide up. Never a route push.
```
"Finish" calls `nextTurn()`, writes the play through the existing roleplay API (so Phase 7 statistics
can count it), and pops to the hub after a one-line sheet: "Scene complete" +
`PrimaryCta("Back to games")`.

## 3. Premium

Premium packs already exist in Phase 4.1 and gate on `isPremiumProvider` (D-5). Premium scenes are
**visible** in the carousel with a `PremiumLockBadge` and route to the paywall on "Choose this scene".
Never a dead tap, never hidden entirely.

## 4. Exit Gate

- [ ] Carousel shows one centred card with scaled and faded neighbours; art fills the card with no
      letterboxing at 360x800 and 430x932; missing art falls back to gradient + glyph.
- [ ] Titles, teasers, roles, beats and durations all come from the existing pack — no new copy.
- [ ] Role assignment always leaves exactly one role per player; "Begin" disabled until picked.
- [ ] Play step shows exactly one beat, one CTA and one speaker chip — never two beats at once.
- [ ] Twist beats reveal on demand only, and no more than the story authored.
- [ ] Beat transitions are fades within one route; Android back exits the scene once.
- [ ] Premium scenes show the badge and route to the paywall; free scenes play through to "Finish".
- [ ] Completing a scene records the play so Phase 7 statistics can read it.
- [ ] analyze clean, build_runner clean, tests green.
- [ ] Commit + PR: `feat(roleplay): passionate roleplay scene flow`.
