## 1. What this game is, in one paragraph

One phone, two people, one turn at a time. The player whose turn it is **flicks a weighted dial** with
their thumb. It lands on one of six position zones. A card rises face-down out of the dial and stays
face-down until they **press and hold** it. It flips to a position illustration with a one-line setup.
They get into it together, tap once, and the card shrinks away to make room for a **beat rail** — the
tempo, drip-fed one beat at a time, big, unrushable. When the last beat is done the turn passes, the
heat rises one notch, and the dial is locked for eight seconds. Then the other person flicks.

**The three things that make it work, all mandatory:**

1. **One player acts per round.** This is not a game both people play simultaneously. The active player
   is the **Leader**: they flick, they reveal, they initiate. The passive player is the **Partner**.
   Roles swap on `nextTurn()`. Everything on screen is addressed to the Leader.
2. **Nothing arrives for free.** Flick to spin, hold to reveal, tap to advance. Three separate physical
   commitments per round. Remove any one of them and the game becomes a randomiser with a picture.
3. **The pacing is enforced in code, not suggested in copy.** A 1200ms minimum dwell per beat, a 900ms
   hold to reveal, and an 8s cooldown between rounds. "Slow" is a set of timers, not an adjective.

### Why a dial and not a wheel

The wheel already exists (4.5.3, merged). A second wheel makes the app look like one idea reskinned
six times. More importantly a wheel's **segments are the outcomes** — with 36 positions and
illustrations, segment art is unreadable at 320px, and if you put categories in the petals the wheel
is doing a pointer's job with worse ergonomics. A pointer scales to any pool size.

And a wheel is `tap -> watch -> read`. A flicked dial is `touch -> throw -> watch`. The player's own
hand makes the motion, so no two spins feel the same. That is the difference between a slot machine
and foreplay.

### Why the spin engine is shared, and what "shared" means

`games_overhaul/PHASE_9_SPIN_THE_BOTTLE.md` specifies a near-identical spin: flick velocity to turn
count, uniform random landing, throttled tick haptics, damped wobble, landing-accuracy assertion,
reduce-motion collapse. Writing that twice is how the two games drift apart and both end up buggy.

**Share the math and the feel. Do not share the artwork.**

| Shared (`lib/core/spin/`) | Owned per game |
|---|---|
| `SpinSolution`, `SpinSolver.solve()` | the painter (bottle vs needle) |
| `zoneCount` as a parameter (6 here, 8 for the bottle) | the ring pucks, palette and icons |
| velocity -> turns mapping, weak-flick rejection | the outcome surface (prompt sheet vs position card) |
| tick-haptic throttle, wobble chain, lifecycle cancel | the copy and the content pack |
| reduce-motion resolved-angle path | seam doubles (bottle only, `seamChance: 0` here) |

Phase 9's `bottle_spin_solver.dart` is **deleted from that plan** and replaced by a call into
`lib/core/spin/spin_solver.dart` with `zoneCount: 8, seamChance: 0.08`. That amendment is already
written into the Phase 9 file. Spin the Bottle has not been implemented yet, so there is no migration
cost — this is why 4.5.5 lands first.

The two games stay distinct because the **object and the payload** differ: the bottle points at
verbal/foreplay zones (kiss, whisper, tease) and returns a one-line instruction; the dial points at
position zones and returns an illustrated card plus a tempo script. If, during review, they still feel
like the same game, cut Spin the Bottle and fold its 8 zones in here as a second mode. Do not ship two
spinners that feel identical.

## 3. Turn model: Leader and Partner

No new session model. Derive both roles from the existing `GameSession` (4.5.1 §3):

```dart
// lib/features/positions/domain/intimacy_role.dart
// Pure projection. No state of its own.
Player leaderOf(GameSession s)  => s.active;
Player partnerOf(GameSession s) => s.passive;
```

- `TurnChipBar` renders unchanged (active 100%, partner 45%). This sub-phase adds **one** suffix line
  inside the existing bar, `chipLabel` at `textSecondary`: `"<Leader> leads"`. Nothing else. Do not
  add a second row, a badge, or a legend.
- Every string the game shows is addressed to the Leader in the second person.
- **`nextTurn()` is called exactly once per round, on `"Done"` at the end of the beat rail.** Not on the
  flick, not on the reveal, not on `"Pass"`, not on `"Spin again"`. Same contract as every other game.

### Name tokens (required)

Every seeded `setup` and closing line may contain **at most one** token, resolved at render:

```
{partner}   -> partnerOf(session).name
{you}       -> leaderOf(session).name
```

Rendered through one helper, `resolveTokens(String, GameSession)`. Reasons this is mandatory, not a
nice-to-have: it personalises every line (the single cheapest intimacy lever in the app), and it keeps
the pack free of assumed genders and assumed anatomy, so one pack serves every couple. A test asserts
no seeded string contains a token outside that set, and that no `{` survives rendering.

### The ring is not always six pucks

```
premium && !softened  -> 6 zones, 60° apart   (WILD included)
otherwise             -> 5 zones, 72° apart   (WILD excluded entirely)
```

WILD is **removed from the ring**, never rendered locked, greyed, or with a `PremiumLockBadge`. The
cognitive-load contract forbids a premium row on a play surface, and a lock on a dial you just flicked
is the worst possible moment to sell something. The upsell lives on the hub tile and in the info sheet.

This is exactly why `SpinSolver` takes `zoneCount` as a parameter instead of hardcoding it.

## 5. `PositionDial`

`size = min(screenWidth - 56, 340)`. One square `SizedBox`, laid out as a `Stack`, bottom to top:

**Layer 1 — heat halo.** `RadialGradient([rose @ (6 + heat * 5)%, transparent], radius 0.70 + heat * 0.04)`.
The only place the heat level is expressed as light. It grows all session and never shrinks.

**Layer 2 — plate.** Circle of `size`, `RadialGradient([#FFFFFF @ 7%, transparent], radius 0.92)`,
1px `hairline` rim, inner shadow `0 6 18 #000 @ 30%`.

**Layer 3 — `ZoneRing`.** Pucks centred on a circle of radius `size * 0.40`, at `i * zoneSweep`
clockwise from 12 o'clock.

```
puck idle     46 circle · fill zone @ 16% · border 1.5px zone @ 50% · icon 20 zone
puck dimmed   same, whole puck at 45%          (all non-winning pucks after landing)
puck landed   scale 1.16 (260ms easeOutBack) · fill zone @ 32% · border 1.5px zone ·
              glow blur 18 zone @ 40%
```
Pucks are **icons only, and they never rotate**. The zone name appears exactly once per round, on the
card's zone chip. `Semantics(label: '<Zone> zone')` on each.

**Layer 4 — the needle (`DialPainter`, inside `Transform.rotate`).** Painted, not an asset: crisp at
every size, cannot 404, consistent with the dice and the bottle.

```
rotation origin = the plate centre
needle points to 12 o'clock at rotation 0
length      size * 0.40 forward · size * 0.10 tail behind the hub
shaft       tapered quad, 7px at the hub -> 2px at the tip,
            vertical gradient [#F7E7EF, #C9A6B8]
tip         rose arrowhead, 14 wide x 18 tall, 1px #FFFFFF @ 70% stroke
tail        counterweight circle 14, radial [roseDeep, #5A1030]
specular    #FFFFFF @ 26% stripe along the shaft, 30% of shaft width, blur sigma 1.0
hub         38 circle, radial [rose, roseDeep], 3px white ring, 8px inner dot #FFFFFF @ 85%
shadow      (0, 2) blur 6 #000 @ 40%, drawn inside the rotated layer so it tracks the needle
```

**Layer 5 — idle affordance.** At rest the needle oscillates ±0.8° over `breathe` and the hub glow
pulses (`pulseGlow`). Both stop the instant a pan begins and do not resume until `invite`/`cooldown`.

**Layer 6 — cooldown arc.** Only in `cooldown`: a `rose` stroke-3 arc sweeping 0 -> 2π clockwise around
the hub at radius `size * 0.16` over 8000ms, linear. It is a progress indicator, not decoration —
when it closes, the CTA enables.

## 6. Input

### Flick (primary)

`GestureDetector` on a hit box of `88 x (size * 0.50)` centred on the plate, never under 44 wide:

- `onPanStart` — stop the idle animation, `selectionClick`, record the pointer angle about the centre.
- `onPanUpdate` — rotate the needle **1:1 with the pointer's angular delta**. Direct manipulation: it
  must feel stuck to the thumb. No smoothing, no easing, no clamping.
- `onPanEnd` — compute angular velocity `ω` (rad/s) from the last 100ms of samples.
  - `|ω| < 1.5` -> **rejected**. Spring back 0.4 rad, settle with a short nudge, `selectionClick`,
    change no state, open nothing. A weak flick is a non-event, not an error.
  - otherwise -> `turns = (ω.abs() / 3.2).round().clamp(2, 6)`, `direction = ω.sign`, spin.

### CTA (fallback, always visible)

`PrimaryCta("Spin the dial")` -> `lightImpact` -> `turns = 3 + random.nextInt(3)`, clockwise. Required
for reduce-motion, screen-reader and one-handed use. It is never hidden and never styled as secondary.

## 7. Spin mechanics (`lib/core/spin/`)

The flick decides **how** it spins. `Random` decides **where** it lands. Keep that split: real friction
would bias outcomes toward the zone opposite the flick, and a rigged feel is worse than an honest one.
Put this comment in `spin_solver.dart` so nobody "fixes" it later.

```dart
// lib/core/spin/spin_solver.dart — pure functions, zero Flutter imports, fully unit-testable.
class SpinSolution {
  final double endDegrees;
  final int target;        // zone index under the pointer
  final bool isDouble;     // seam landing; always false when seamChance == 0
}

SpinSolution solve({
  required Random random,
  required int zoneCount,      // 6 or 5 here; 8 for Spin the Bottle
  required int turns,
  required int direction,       // +1 / -1
  double seamChance = 0,        // 0 here; 0.08 for Spin the Bottle
}) {
  final sweep    = 360 / zoneCount;
  final isDouble = seamChance > 0 && random.nextDouble() < seamChance;
  final target   = random.nextInt(zoneCount);
  final offset   = isDouble ? sweep / 2 : 0.0;
  final endDeg   = direction * (turns * 360 + target * sweep + offset);
  return SpinSolution(endDegrees: endDeg, target: target, isDouble: isDouble);
}

int zoneForDegrees(double deg, int zoneCount) {
  final sweep = 360 / zoneCount;
  return ((((deg % 360) + 360) % 360) / sweep).round() % zoneCount;
}
```

- `AnimationController(duration: 4200ms)`, curve `Cubic(0.16, 0.84, 0.04, 1.0)`, chained to a settle
  wobble: two damped oscillations of ±3.5° about the final angle (`easeOutSine`), amplitude halving.
- **Tick haptics** (`spin_tick_haptics.dart`, shared): in the listener compute
  `crossed = (degrees / sweep).floor()`; on change fire `HapticFeedback.selectionClick()`, throttled to
  a 45ms minimum so the fast phase does not machine-gun. Never above ~22/second.
- **Landing:** `heavyImpact`. The winning puck animates to its landed state; all others dim to 45%.
- **Accuracy:** assert the resolved angle maps back to `target` with **< 0.5°** error.
- **Lifecycle:** on `AppLifecycleState.paused` cancel the controller and jump to the resolved angle. On
  resume the state is already final. Never leave a half-spun needle on screen.
- 300ms after the wobble settles, transition `spinning -> held`.

## 8. The card: held, then revealed

### 8.1 `HeldCard` (stage `held`)

```
232 x 320 · radius 22
entry: rises out of the hub — offset y +40 -> 0, scale 0.86 -> 1.00, opacity 0 -> 1,
       420ms easeOutCubic. The dial fades to 35% behind it (220ms) and stops animating.
face : gradient positionDial 135° + inner top-left highlight · 1px border #FFFFFF @ 10% ·
       shadow tile
  zone chip   top, height 24, radius 12, fill zone @ 22%, icon 12 + cardLabel in zone colour
  centre      the zone glyph at 34px, #FFFFFF @ 22%
  bottom      "Press and hold" caption at 70%
```

**The hold:** `onLongPress` with a 900ms threshold. During the press a `rose` stroke-3 progress ring
sweeps 0 -> 2π around the card's centre at radius 132. Release early -> rewind over 200ms, no state
change, no message. Complete -> `mediumImpact`, then flip.

**Flip:** `rotateY` 0 -> π over 620ms (`cardFlip`), perspective `Matrix4..setEntry(3, 2, 0.0012)`. Swap
the child at `t > 0.5` and counter-rotate the front child by π so text is not mirrored.

One phone, one thumb. Do not build a two-finger or two-device ritual — it is fiddly at exactly the
moment nobody wants to be fiddling.

## 9. The beat rail — where "slow" is enforced

On `"We're in position"` the card animates (320ms easeOutCubic) into a header strip and the rail takes
over the hero.

```
CardStrip   height 76 · GlassPanel radius 18 · padding H 14
            44 thumbnail (art or silhouette) · 10 · name promptBody scaled to 15/20 ·
            Spacer · zone chip 24
            The setup line is NOT repeated here. Rule 4: never render the same information twice.
16
BeatRail    owns the remaining hero height
  BreathRing behind, centred: circle diameter 200, stroke 2, rose @ 30%,
             scale 0.86 <-> 1.00 on a 4s inhale (easeInOut) / 6s exhale, repeating.
             One lightImpact at each inhale peak, max one per 4s.
             This is the metronome. It gives two people a shared tempo with no audio,
             which matters because audio does not land until Phase 9.
  current beat, centred over the ring:
     count   heroDisplay at 44/48, rose            "5"
     4
     motion  cardLabel uppercase, textPrimary, letter-spacing 3.0     "SLOW"
     hold beat    -> the numeral is replaced by a 10s countdown ring; motion reads "HOLD"
     closing beat -> no numeral at all; the line renders as promptBody, centred, maxLines 2,
                     tokens resolved
  progress, 20 below: N dashes, 18 x 3, radius 2, filled rose / hairline
```

**Advance:** tap anywhere in the hero. `lightImpact`.

**The dwell floor:** a tap inside **1200ms** of the beat appearing is ignored. No error, no toast, no
shake — the current dash pulses once and nothing else happens. This single rule is the difference
between a game people rush through in ninety seconds and one that takes twenty minutes. Do not make it
configurable and do not shorten it for "snappiness".

The last beat swaps the tap-to-advance for `PrimaryCta("Done")` -> `nextTurn()` -> `cooldown`.

### 9.1 What is actually written on a beat

**Answer to "3 thrusts, or just try this position?" — both, in that order, never one alone.**

Counts alone read like a rep counter in a fitness app: clinical, and the fantasy dies. "Just try this
position" alone is a suggestion, not a game, and it stops producing new experiences by round three.
The structure that works is: the **card** says *what* (position + setup), the **beats** say *how much
and how fast* (count + motion), and the **final beat is always a human line with no number** so the
round ends on a person, not on arithmetic.

```dart
// lib/features/positions/domain/beat_motion.dart
enum BeatMotion { slow, deep, shallow, grind, tease, still, hold, kiss, whisper }

// lib/features/positions/domain/tempo_beat.dart
class TempoBeat {
  final int? count;        // null for hold and closing beats
  final BeatMotion? motion;// null for closing beats
  final String? line;      // non-null only for the closing beat
}
```

The word "thrust" appears **nowhere in shipped copy**. It is clinical, it assumes one anatomy, and
copy rule §11 rules it out. Motion words carry the same information and read better: `SLOW`, `DEEP`,
`SHALLOW`, `GRIND`, `TEASE`, `STILL`. If a user needs the concept spelled out, the info sheet does it
once, in one sentence.

### 9.2 `BeatScriptBuilder` (pure, no Flutter imports)

Scripts are **generated per round**, not authored per position. 36 positions x 5 heat levels of hand
written scripts is 180 blocks nobody will maintain, and it makes a position feel identical every time
it comes up. Generation means the same position at heat 2 and at heat 5 are different experiences.

```dart
BeatScript build({
  required Random random,
  required PositionZone zone,
  required int heat,          // 1..5
});
```

| heat | beats | count range | motions available |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 3–5 | `slow`, `still`, `kiss` |
| 2 | 3 | 4–6 | + `tease`, `shallow` |
| 3 | 3 | 5–8 | + `deep` |
| 4 | 4 | 6–10 | + `grind` |
| 5 | 5 | 8–14 | all |

Invariants the builder guarantees and the test asserts:

- The **last** beat is always a closing line: `count == null`, `motion == null`, `line != null`, drawn
  from that zone's closing pool.
- At `heat >= 3` there is **exactly one** `hold` beat. It is never first and never last.
- No two consecutive beats share the same motion.
- Counts are monotonically non-decreasing across a script — the round escalates inside itself.
- Total beats match the table exactly.

## 10. Escalation, cooldown, afterglow — the retention loop

### 10.1 Heat ladder (pure)

```dart
// lib/features/positions/domain/heat_ladder.dart
int heatFor(int completedRounds, {required bool premium, required bool softened}) {
  final raw = 1 + (completedRounds ~/ 2);
  final cap = softened ? 2 : (premium ? 5 : 3);
  return raw.clamp(1, cap);
}
```

Heat rises every two completed rounds and **never falls inside a session**. It drives four things at
once: which positions are eligible (`position.heatMin <= heat`), beat count, count range, and the
brightness of the dial's heat halo. It resets only when the screen is left.

That is the loop: you flick again to find out how much further it goes. Nothing else in the app has a
reason to keep going past round three.

### 10.2 `HeatMeter`

Five pips, 6px circles, 6 apart, top-right of the `TurnChipBar` row. Filled `rose`, unfilled
`hairline`. On a level-up the newly filled pip scales 1.0 -> 1.4 -> 1.0 over 320ms and fires
`mediumImpact`. **No label, no number, no percentage** — it is a temperature you feel, not a score.
Capped pips beyond the cap are simply not drawn (free users see three pips, not three of five).

### 10.3 Cooldown gate

8000ms. `PrimaryCta("Spin again")` sits at 38% while the arc from §5 Layer 6 draws. Footnote reads
"Stay close." and disappears when the arc closes.

Escape hatches, both required: reduce-motion users skip the gate entirely, and a long-press on the
disabled CTA releases it after 2000ms. Pacing should be the path of least resistance, not a cage.

### 10.4 `AfterglowSheet`

Opens on back/home from `cooldown` when `completedRounds >= 2`, and automatically after round 8.

```
ResultSheet:
  "That was a good one."                    screenTitle
  14
  heat pips (final state) + "Round N"       chipLabel textSecondary, one line
  16
  the positions played, as chips            wrapped, max 8, zone-coloured, name only.
                                            No scroll. No list widget. If more than 8, show the
                                            last 8 — this is a memento, not history.
  Spacer
  PrimaryCta("Keep tonight")   -> writes the round's positions to the existing favourites API, pops
  SecondaryTextButton("Close") -> pops
```

Under 2 rounds, no sheet — pop straight out. Never congratulate someone for one round.

### 10.5 Consent, once

Reuse `ConsentSheet` from 4.5.4 verbatim, shown on the **first entry to this screen ever** (flag in
`game_prefs_box`), before the dial is interactive:

```

"Tap pass on any card. No score, no streak lost."   caption textSecondary
20
PrimaryCta("Got it")
SecondaryTextButton("Soften it")   -> sets the pref: heat cap 2, WILD off, re-renders a 5-zone ring
```

This is the most explicit content in the app. The sheet is a UX requirement, and it is also what makes
the store listing and the Bible §14 posture defensible.

## 11. Content pack — 36 positions, 48 closing lines

**ADR D-11 — Phase 4.5 ships new content, once, here.**
Context: Phase 4.5 is declared a presentation layer that reuses Phase 3/4 packs (D-9). This sub-phase
has no pack to reuse: `lib/features/positions/` is an empty stub and Position Library is flag-gated
off (D-3). Decision: authorise exactly one new pack, closed at 36 positions and 48 closing lines,
seeded as immutable JSON with a `seedVersion`, adding no Hive adapters. Consequence: D-3 stays as it
is — this is a **game**, not the Position Library, and it does not unflag that feature.

> **Id note:** this is **D-11**, not D-10. D-10 is already assigned in `VELOURA_BUILD_PLAN.md` to
> "Two-player session state is shared, not per-game". Verified against the registry before numbering.

Record D-11 in `VELOURA_BUILD_PLAN.md` in this PR, alongside a one-line amendment to D-9 naming the
exception, so the ADR lands atomically with the code it authorises.

### Draw rules

- Eligible pool = `zone == landed && heatMin <= heat && (isPremium ? entitled : true) && !usedThisSession`.
- `usedIds` is a per-session `Set<String>`. When one zone's pool empties, reset **that zone only**.
- If the eligible pool is empty at the current heat (possible at heat 1 in a zone whose low-heat
  entries are used), fall back to the lowest `heatMin` available in that zone rather than re-spinning.
  The needle already landed; moving it would read as a bug.
