## 2. `SpinWheel` rendering

- `CustomPaint` inside `Transform.rotate(angle: rotationRadians)`. Ten segments of 36 degrees.
- **Pinwheel petals, not pie slices.** Per segment: `moveTo(center)` ->
  `arcTo(outerRect, startAngle, sweep)` -> `quadraticBezierTo(control, center)`, where the control
  point sits at `radius * 0.55` rotated `+13` degrees from the segment's start angle. That curve is
  what produces the swirl.
- Segment icons at `radius * 0.66` inside a 30px circle `#FFFFFF @ 92%` with a 1px `#00000014` border,
  icon 16 in `#8E2A63`. Icons are **counter-rotated by `-rotation`** so they stay upright while the
  wheel turns. Order:
  `[casino, water_drop, arrow_forward, music_note, lips.svg, star, casino, flame.svg, water_drop, arrow_forward]`
- Rim: 6px ring `#FFFFFF @ 85%` outside the petals plus an outer glow `rose @ 22%` blur 24.
- Hub: 34px circle, radial `[rose, roseDeep]`, 3px white ring, 8px inner dot `#FFFFFF @ 85%`.
- Pointer: fixed at 12 o'clock above the wheel — rounded triangle 20 wide x 15 tall, fill `rose`,
  1px `#FFFFFF @ 70%` stroke, shadow `(0, 2)` blur 6 `#000 @ 40%`.
- Idle: wheel breathes scale 1.000 <-> 1.012 (`breathe`); the hub glow pulses (`pulseGlow`).

## 3. Spin mechanics

```dart
// Segment parity defines the type: even index => DARE, odd index => TRUTH (5 and 5).
final target        = random.nextInt(10);
final segmentCentre = target * 36 + 18;                     // degrees
final turns         = 4 + random.nextInt(3);                // 4..6 full rotations
final endDegrees    = turns * 360 + (360 - segmentCentre);  // segment lands under the pointer
// AnimationController(duration: 4200ms), curve Cubic(0.12, 0.78, 0.06, 1.0)
```
- Tick haptics: in the listener compute `crossed = (degrees / 36).floor()`; on change fire
  `HapticFeedback.selectionClick()`, throttled to a minimum of 45ms so the fast phase does not
  machine-gun.
- Landing: `heavyImpact`; the winning petal flashes `#FFFFFF @ 30%` fading over 260ms and the rim glow
  rises briefly to `rose @ 45%`.
- Accuracy: assert `endDegrees % 360` maps back to `target` with under 0.5 degrees of error.
- The **prompt is drawn from the existing repository** once the type is known:
  `truthDareRepository.getRandom(type: resolvedType, difficulty: activeFilters)`. The wheel decides
  Truth vs Dare; the repository decides which item, honouring the existing no-repeat rolling window.
- 300ms after landing, open the result sheet.

## 5. Difficulty and premium mapping

- The existing `Difficulty` enum drives heat. `extreme` items are only in the pool when the
  "Superhot Roulette" premium flag is on, gated through `isPremiumProvider` (D-5).
- Filtered-out items are **excluded from the pool**, never rendered greyed or locked.
- "Soften decks" in `GamePreferencesSheet` drops `extreme` regardless of premium.
